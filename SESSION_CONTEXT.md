### Wake word — replaced August 2026

Picovoice Porcupine's free tier was terminated 30 June 2026, so it was replaced with
**sherpa-onnx** keyword spotting (Apache-2.0, fully on-device, no key). The model binaries are
committed under `carlsbrain/src/main/assets/kws/`.

**`docs/wake-word.md` is the authority** — read it before touching anything wake-word related.
It documents two silent-failure traps (the `bpe.model` is sentencepiece UNIGRAM despite its
name; `keywords.txt` must be UTF-8/no-BOM/LF) and why the coordinate is
`com.github.k2-fsa.sherpa-onnx:sherpa-onnx` and not the repo root.

Field-tested by Carl: zero false activations across a 30-minute meeting, reliable triggering
on "Hey Brain" at the model default threshold. Battery cost noticeable but acceptable.

## Known-good invariants — do not regress these

- **Vault items must never appear in notifications.** Notifications render on the lock screen,
  outside the biometric gate. A voice-saved vault item leaked its title this way and was fixed
  in `a1d6d64`. `searchTodos` and the widget queries already exclude vault buckets.
- **Vault buckets are excluded from unprotected export or share.**
- **Post-save work must not run in `viewModelScope`** — navigation clears it mid-flight and the
  work is silently cancelled. Use `CarlsBrainApp.appScope`. This is why Claude auto-tagging
  once never ran, and why the zip export was truncating.
- **Bucket deletion must reassign rows off the bucket before deleting it**, inside a
  transaction. Soft-delete alone is not enough: the rows keep `bucketId`, so the FK CASCADE
  removes them from Recently Deleted too.
- **`save()` must use `existing.copy(...)`**, never `TodoEntity.fromDomain(...)`, which drops
  `isArchived`, `deletedAt`, `sortOrder`, `attachments` and more.

## Recently fixed — context for anything odd Carl reports

- **A voice-created todo could be completed by the same Claude reply that created it.**
  `[DONE:]` runs after `[TODO:]` on one response and matches by substring, so a brand-new todo
  was a candidate. Once done, the nightly cleanup archived it — leaving an item that opened
  fine but never appeared in the list. Fixed in `36a5c28`; `[DONE:]` now also names what it
  completed and asks when ambiguous (`2cdf8b8`).
- **Archived and soft-deleted todos still load by id**, so the editor opened them looking
  normal while the list excluded them. The editor now says so and offers restore (`8ea088c`).
- **An invisible 48dp delete target** covered half a 72dp attachment thumbnail (`7a27c9e`).
